What Section 12(1)(c) requires

Private unaided schools, and specified-category schools, must admit children from weaker sections and disadvantaged groups in the neighbourhood to at least 25% of the strength of the entry class. The children study free until they complete elementary education (Class 8).

Reserved seats = 25% of entry-class seats, rounded up

How reimbursement works

Under Section 12(2), the state reimburses the school the lower of the state’s per-child expenditure and the fee the school actually charges. Each state notifies its per-child amount, often every year, and some states also pay for uniforms and books.

Who counts as weaker sections and disadvantaged groups

States define these groups in their RTE rules. They typically include SC and ST children, children from families below a notified income limit, orphans, children with disabilities, and children affected by HIV. Admission is usually by lottery through a state portal.
