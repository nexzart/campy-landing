What the analysis shows

  • Pass percentage: students who reached the pass mark in every subject, as a share of students who appeared.
  • Class average: the mean of every student’s overall percentage.
  • Rank: by total marks. Students with equal totals share a rank.
  • Subject pass %: students at or above the pass mark in that subject, out of those who appeared. Absent students are left out of the subject’s averages.
  • Grade: each student’s overall percentage on the CBSE 8-point scale.

Using it in the staff meeting

Subjects with a pass percentage below 75% or a low average usually need a remedial plan. Students who failed one or two subjects are the ones to follow up with first, and the toppers list is ready for the notice board.
