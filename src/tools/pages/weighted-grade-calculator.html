How a weighted grade is calculated

Weighted grade = Σ (component % × weight) ÷ Σ weights

Each assessment is first turned into a percentage, so a 17 out of 20 test counts as 85%. Each percentage is then multiplied by its weight before the results are added up.

A typical CBSE scheme

For Classes 9 and 10, CBSE splits each subject into 80 marks for the board or annual exam and 20 for internal assessment. The internal part covers periodic tests, the notebook and portfolio, and subject enrichment. Schools set their own weights for Classes 6 to 8 and for term exams.
