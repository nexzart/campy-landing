Percentage to CGPA formula

CGPA = percentage ÷ 9.5

85.5% ÷ 9.5 = 9.0 CGPA. 76% ÷ 9.5 = 8.0 CGPA.

Where the 9.5 comes from

Under the CCE scheme, CBSE found that students graded A1 (91–100) scored an average of about 95. A CGPA of 10 was therefore taken as 95%, giving a factor of 9.5.

When not to use 9.5

Universities and other boards often use their own conversions, such as dividing by 10 or a published table. If your institution states a formula, use that factor in the calculator instead.
