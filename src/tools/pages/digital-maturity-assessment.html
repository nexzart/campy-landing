How the score is calculated

Each answer scores 0 (Not yet) to 3 (Fully). An area’s score is its points as a share of the 12 possible, and the overall score is the average of the five areas.

ScoreLevelWhat it usually looks like
0–29%BeginningRegisters and paper files, with communication through diaries and notices
30–54%DevelopingSome software and messaging groups, used separately
55–79%EstablishedCore work is digital and connected; parents get updates on their phones
80–100%LeadingData guides decisions, and every user has their own secure login

Where to start

For most schools, the fastest gain is moving administration (records, attendance, fees and marks) into one system. Communication and data security improve along with it.
