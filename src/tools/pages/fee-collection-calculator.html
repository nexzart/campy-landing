At % collection, about of fees would still be due at year end. The average fee due per student is a year.

How expected fee collection is worked out

  1. Gross billing: for each fee head, amount × times a year × students × share of students paying.
  2. Concessions: gross × share of students with a concession × average concession.
  3. Net due: gross − concessions.
  4. Expected collection: net × collection rate. This is the figure to budget salaries and expenses against.

Setting a realistic collection rate

Look at last year: divide what you actually collected by what was due, and use that rate. Schools that send reminders before each due date, and let parents see pending fees in an app, usually collect more on time.

Concessions to include

  • Sibling and staff-ward concessions
  • Merit and sports scholarships paid from school funds
  • RTE Section 12(1)(c) admissions, if state reimbursement arrives late or below your fee
