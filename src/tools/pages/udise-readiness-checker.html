What UDISE+ collects

Every recognised school in India submits UDISE+ data each year through the official portal. Collection usually runs in the second half of the year, with timelines notified by the Ministry of Education and your state. The data covers:

  • School profile: location, management, affiliation, classes offered and medium of instruction.
  • Facilities: classrooms and their condition, toilets, drinking water, electricity, library, ramps, computers and internet.
  • Teachers: one record per teacher, with qualifications, appointment, classes taught and training.
  • Students: one record per student, with progression from the previous year, PEN, and exam results.
  • Finance: receipts and expenditure, and fees for private schools.

How the score works

Each item has a weight of 1 to 3. Critical items, such as complete student records and an active UDISE+ login, count three times as much as minor ones. The score is the weighted share of items you have ticked. It is a preparation aid, not an official assessment.

The most common hold-ups

  • Missing or mismatched dates of birth and Aadhaar details, which stop PEN generation
  • Students who left during the year but are still on the rolls
  • Teacher qualifications not recorded
