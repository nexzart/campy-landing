What goes on a school fee receipt

  • A unique receipt number and the date of payment
  • The student’s name, class, admission number and parent’s name
  • The period the payment covers, such as a term, quarter or month
  • Each fee head paid, any concession or discount, and any late fee
  • The total in figures and in words, in the Indian format
  • The payment mode, with the UPI, cheque or transaction reference

Amount in words

Amounts are written in lakhs and crores: ₹1,12,500 becomes “Rupees One Lakh Twelve Thousand Five Hundred Only”. Paise are added when the amount has them.

Keeping receipts in order

Number receipts in sequence for each financial year, such as 2026/0001, and keep the school copy. Fee audits and parent disputes are much easier when every number is accounted for.
