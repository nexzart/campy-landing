How the PGT salary is calculated

Basic pay starts at ₹47,600 and moves one cell down Level 8 with each annual increment. Dearness allowance, house rent allowance by city class, and transport allowance are added to it. NPS (10% of basic pay + DA), professional tax and other deductions are then taken off to give the in-hand salary.

Gross = basic + DA + HRA + TA + DA on TA

Typical PGT qualification

For Kendriya Vidyalaya and Navodaya Vidyalaya recruitment, a PGT usually needs a master’s degree with at least 50% marks in the subject concerned and a B.Ed. Each recruitment notice gives the exact requirement.

Pay in state and private schools

State governments have their own pay commissions and scales, and private schools set their own salaries. The 7th CPC figure is a useful benchmark when you compare offers or set a salary structure. For other posts, use the teacher salary calculator with any pay level.
