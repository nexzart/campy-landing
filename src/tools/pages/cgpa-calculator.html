Equivalent percentage (× 9.5)

A subject graded E1 or E2 has no grade point. Under CCE, the student had to improve that grade before a final CGPA was awarded.

How CGPA is calculated

CGPA = sum of grade points ÷ number of subjects

Grades A1, A2, A2, B1 and A1 give 10 + 9 + 9 + 8 + 10 = 46, and 46 ÷ 5 = 9.2 CGPA, or about 87.4%.
