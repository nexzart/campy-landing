Building your academic calendar

  1. Choose the month and year your session starts. April is most common for CBSE and many state boards. Some states and international schools start in June.
  2. Choose your Saturday rule.
  3. List gazetted holidays, vacations, exam windows and events, one per line.
  4. Check the working days per month, and the total against your board’s and RTE minimums (200 days for Classes 1–5, 220 for Classes 6–8).

Accepted date formats

Dates can be written as 2026-08-15 or 15/08/2026. A range uses “to”: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-23 | holiday | Diwali break.

Exam and event days count as working days. Holidays and weekly offs don’t.
