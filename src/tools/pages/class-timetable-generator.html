How the timetable is generated

The generator fills the week one day at a time. For each slot it picks the subject with the most periods left, avoiding:

  • using a subject more times in one day than its weekly periods divided by the remaining days, and
  • placing the same subject in two periods back to back.

Ties are broken at random, so each press of Generate gives a different, equally balanced timetable.

Good timetable habits

  • Put languages and Mathematics in the morning, when attention is highest.
  • Keep Physical Education away from the period just after lunch.
  • Use double periods for practicals and art by placing the same subject in two slots by hand.
