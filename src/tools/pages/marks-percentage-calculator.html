How to calculate percentage from marks

Percentage = (total marks obtained ÷ total maximum marks) × 100

With 430 out of 500, the percentage is 430 ÷ 500 × 100 = 86%. When subjects have different maximum marks, add the marks and the maximums separately before dividing. Don’t average the subject percentages.

Best-of-five percentage

CBSE Class 10 and 12 students often take six subjects. Many colleges and the CBSE merit certificate use the five best. The calculator picks the five subjects with the highest percentage. Some colleges require English among the five, so check the admission rules.

Grades shown

Each subject is graded on the CBSE 8-point scale: A1 91–100, A2 81–90, B1 71–80, B2 61–70, C1 51–60, C2 41–50, D 33–40, E below 33.
