CBSE’s factor is 9.5. Change it if your university uses another.

CGPA to percentage table

CGPA to percentage formula

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

A CGPA of 8.6 is 8.6 × 9.5 = 81.7%. A CGPA of 9.2 is 87.4%.

Subject-wise percentage

CBSE also allowed an indicative subject percentage from its grade point: grade point × 9.5. An A2 (grade point 9) is about 85.5%.

Why the result is approximate

A grade covers a band of ten marks. A1 means anything from 91 to 100, so a converted percentage is an estimate, not the student’s actual marks. When a form asks for exact marks, use the mark sheet.
