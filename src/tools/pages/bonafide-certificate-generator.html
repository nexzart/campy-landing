When students need a bonafide certificate

  • Scholarship applications, including the National Scholarship Portal and state schemes
  • Opening a bank account for a minor
  • Passport and Aadhaar applications
  • Student bus or railway concession passes
  • Registering for Olympiads, competitive exams and sports events

What the certificate should say

It confirms the student is currently enrolled, and gives the class, section, academic year, admission number and date of birth as recorded by the school. It also states the purpose it is issued for. The tool writes these sentences for you and uses the right pronouns for the student.

Before signing, check the details against the admission register, and record the certificate number in your issue register.
