How the teacher requirement is calculated

Teachers for a subject = (sections × periods a week) ÷ max periods per teacher, rounded up

18 sections with 7 English periods each make 126 periods a week. At 30 periods per teacher, English needs 126 ÷ 30 = 4.2, so 5 teachers.

Choosing a teaching load

  • A 6-day week of 8 periods has 48 slots. A load of 28 to 32 periods leaves time for correction, planning and substitution duty.
  • Senior secondary teachers with practicals often take fewer theory periods.
  • Class teachers often have a lighter subject load to make room for registers and parent meetings.

Why add a leave reserve

On any given day, some teachers are away on leave, training or exam duty. A 5–10% reserve keeps substitutions from overloading the rest of the staff.
