What an inspection committee looks at

CBSE inspections for affiliation, upgrading or extension check that the school meets the Affiliation Bye-Laws. This checklist groups the requirements into five areas:

  • Documents and recognition: registration, land, state recognition, and the building, fire and water and sanitation certificates.
  • Staff: qualified principal and teachers in enough numbers, paid as per norms through the bank, with statutory benefits.
  • Infrastructure: classrooms, labs, library, playground and barrier-free access.
  • Safety and welfare: CCTV, POCSO compliance, transport safety, first aid and fire preparedness.
  • Academics and governance: curriculum, timetable, records and the School Management Committee.

Mandatory public disclosure

CBSE schools must publish their mandatory public disclosure (Appendix IX) on the school website. It covers general information, documents, results, staff and infrastructure. Inspectors and CBSE check it, and an outdated disclosure is one of the most common gaps.

This is a self-assessment, not a CBSE score. Always prepare from the current Affiliation Bye-Laws and the latest CBSE circulars.
