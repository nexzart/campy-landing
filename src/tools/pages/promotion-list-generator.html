How each student is placed

  1. Detained for attendance if attendance is below the minimum you set.
  2. Promoted if no subject is failed and the overall percentage meets the minimum.
  3. Compartment if the overall minimum is met and the failed subjects are within the number allowed for a supplementary exam.
  4. Detained in every other case.

Rules to check before you publish

  • CBSE Classes 10 and 12: candidates who fail in up to two subjects may take the supplementary exam. Your board’s current rules apply for other classes.
  • Classes 1 to 8: the 2019 RTE amendment lets states hold exams in Classes 5 and 8 and detain students who fail a re-examination. Many states do not detain at all. Follow your state’s notification.
  • Attendance shortfalls can often be condoned for medical or other valid reasons. Record the reason in the remarks before printing.
