How working days are counted

Every day from the first to the last day of the session is counted once. Sundays and the Saturdays you mark as off are weekly offs. Holidays and vacations falling on other days are holidays. Every remaining day is a working day.

Working days = calendar days − weekly offs − holidays on other days
