How students are seated

Each room is filled column by column, front to back. Every column takes the next class in turn, so the students on either side of a candidate are from different classes and are writing different papers. Classes continue from one room to the next, and the door list for each room shows the roll number ranges seated there.

Getting the most from the plan

  • Mix at least two classes per room. With only one class, neighbours will share a paper.
  • Count only usable seats. A two-seater bench is two seats in a row.
  • Paste each room’s door list outside the room on exam morning so students find their room quickly.
