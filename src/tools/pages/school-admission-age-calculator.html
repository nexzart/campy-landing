How admission age is worked out

A child’s completed age on the cut-off date decides the class. For a 31 March cut-off and the 2026–27 session, a child must be born on or before 31 March 2020 to join Class 1 at 6+.

Eligible class = the highest class whose minimum age the child has completed on the cut-off date

Age by class under NEP 2020

NEP 2020’s 5+3+3+4 structure puts three years of pre-school (ages 3 to 6) before Class 1, so Class 1 starts at age 6:

  • Nursery or Balvatika 1: 3+
  • LKG or Balvatika 2: 4+
  • UKG or Balvatika 3: 5+
  • Class 1: 6+

The Ministry of Education has asked all states and union territories to apply the 6+ rule for Class 1. Some states phase it in, and cut-off dates differ. Always confirm with your state’s current admission notification.
