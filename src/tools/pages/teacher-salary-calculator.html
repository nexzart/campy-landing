How a 7th Pay Commission salary is built

  1. Basic pay comes from the pay matrix: the entry pay for the level, raised 3% for each increment and rounded to the nearest ₹100.
  2. Dearness allowance (DA) is a percentage of basic pay, revised twice a year (January and July).
  3. House rent allowance (HRA) is 30%, 20% or 10% of basic pay for X, Y and Z cities, with minimums of ₹5,400, ₹3,600 and ₹1,800.
  4. Transport allowance (TA) is a fixed amount by pay level and city, plus DA on it.
Gross = basic + DA + HRA + TA + DA on TA

Transport allowance rates

  • Level 9 and above: ₹7,200 in higher TPTA cities, ₹3,600 elsewhere.
  • Levels 3 to 8: ₹3,600 in higher TPTA cities, ₹1,800 elsewhere.
  • Levels 1 and 2: ₹1,350 and ₹900, or the Level 3–8 rates when pay is ₹24,200 or more.
