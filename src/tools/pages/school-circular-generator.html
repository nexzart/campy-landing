How to write a clear school circular

  1. Subject line: say what it is about in a few words, such as “Parent–Teacher Meeting on 14 November”.
  2. First paragraph: what is happening, when and where.
  3. Second paragraph: what parents or staff need to do, and by when.
  4. Close: the name and designation of the person issuing it.

Give every circular a reference number from your register, such as CIR/2026–27/054, so parents and staff can refer to it later.

Getting circulars to parents

A printed circular often stays in a school bag. Sending the same notice to parents’ phones, and asking them to acknowledge it, means you know who has read it.
