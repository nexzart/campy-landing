How the report card is calculated

  • Subject total: Term 1 + Term 2 marks.
  • Subject percentage: subject total ÷ (Term 1 maximum + Term 2 maximum) × 100.
  • Grade: the subject percentage on the CBSE 8-point scale.
  • Overall percentage: all marks ÷ all maximum marks × 100.

Writing useful remarks

Good remarks name one strength and one specific next step, in words a parent can act on. “Reads fluently; should practise long division at home” helps more than “Good, can do better”.
