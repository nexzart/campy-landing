The formula

Final score needed = (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight

Weight is written as a fraction: 40% is 0.4. With a current grade of 78%, a final worth 40% and a target of 80%: (80 − 78 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = (80 − 46.8) ÷ 0.4 = 83%.

Getting the current grade right

Your current grade must be the weighted average of everything except the final exam. If you only know individual scores, use the weighted grade calculator first.
