How the timings are worked out

The day starts with assembly. Each period runs for the set length, a break is added after the period you choose, and passing time is added between periods that aren’t followed by a break.

Checking against instructional hours

Multiply the daily teaching time by your working days to check the year’s instructional hours. For example, 5 h 20 min a day over 220 days is about 1,173 hours, above the RTE minimum of 1,000 hours for Classes 6–8.
