What a school transfer certificate contains

The format follows the transfer certificate most CBSE and state board schools use. It records:

  • Identity: name, parents’ names, nationality, category, and date of birth in figures and words, as in the admission register.
  • School history: date and class of first admission, class last studied, the last exam and its result, and whether the pupil qualifies for promotion.
  • Attendance and dues: working days, days present, and the month up to which fees are paid.
  • Conduct and activities: NCC or Scouts and Guides, games, and general conduct.
  • Leaving details: dates of application, removal from the rolls and issue, and the reason for leaving.

Issuing the TC

Print the certificate on the school’s letterhead or paper, have it checked against the admission register, and have the principal sign and stamp it. Record the TC number in the school’s TC register. For CBSE schools in Classes 9 to 12, also check the board’s rules on mid-session transfers.
