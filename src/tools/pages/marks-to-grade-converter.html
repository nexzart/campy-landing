Grading scales included

CBSE 8-point scale

A1 91–100, A2 81–90, B1 71–80, B2 61–70, C1 51–60, C2 41–50, D 33–40 and E 32 and below (needs improvement). CBSE uses this scale for school-based assessment.

CCE 9-point scale

The older CCE scale splits E into E1 (21–32) and E2 (0–20). It is still used by schools that report CGPA.

How marks become a grade

Marks are converted to a percentage of the maximum, rounded to two decimals, and matched to the highest band whose minimum the student reaches. A student with 58 out of 80 has 72.5%, which is grade B1.
