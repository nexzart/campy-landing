How the pupil–teacher ratio is calculated

Pupil–teacher ratio = students enrolled ÷ full-time teachers

A school with 640 students and 19 teachers has a ratio of 33.7 : 1. To get under 30 : 1, it needs 22 teachers (640 ÷ 30 = 21.3, rounded up).

Norms used in this calculator

RTE Act, 2009 (Schedule)

  • Classes 1–5: 2 teachers for up to 60 children, 3 for 61–90, 4 for 91–120 and 5 for 121–200, with a head teacher above 150. Above 200 children, the ratio excluding the head teacher must not exceed 40 : 1.
  • Classes 6–8: at least one teacher per class, so that there is a teacher each for science and mathematics, social studies, and languages, and at least one teacher for every 35 children.

CBSE affiliation

CBSE expects a pupil–teacher ratio of no more than 30 : 1, 1.5 teachers for every section, and no more than 40 students in a section. The calculator uses whichever of the 30 : 1 and 1.5-per-section figures is higher.

NEP 2020

The National Education Policy aims for a ratio under 30 : 1 in every school, and under 25 : 1 in areas with many socio-economically disadvantaged students.

Norms are revised from time to time. Check the current RTE rules for your state and your board’s latest affiliation bye-laws before an inspection.
