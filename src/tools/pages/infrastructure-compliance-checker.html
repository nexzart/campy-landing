Norms used by default

  • Classrooms: RTE requires at least one classroom for every teacher. CBSE expects a room for every section.
  • Classroom size: CBSE norms call for at least 8 m × 6 m (48 m², about 517 sq ft). The default threshold of 500 sq ft is a rounded figure; raise it if your inspector applies the full metric size.
  • Space per student: at least 1 m² (10.76 sq ft) per student in a classroom.
  • Section size: CBSE caps a section at 40 students.
  • Toilets: RTE and CBSE require separate toilets for boys and girls. The students-per-toilet figure is a planning target, so set it to your state’s guideline.

Norms differ between boards, states and school categories, and are revised over time. Treat this as a first check, and confirm against the rules that apply to your school.
