How groups are made

Names are shuffled with a Fisher–Yates shuffle, then dealt into groups like playing cards. Group sizes never differ by more than one: 22 students in groups of 4 give four groups of 4 and two groups of 3.

Ideas for using random groups

  • Project teams and lab partners that change every term
  • Quiz teams and debate sides
  • Picking who answers next, so every student stays ready
