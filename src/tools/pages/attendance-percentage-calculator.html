How attendance percentage is calculated

Divide the days a student was present by the total working days, then multiply by 100.

Attendance % = (days present ÷ total working days) × 100

A student present on 168 of 210 working days has 168 ÷ 210 × 100 = 80% attendance.

Days needed to reach 75%

Days needed = (0.75 × days held − days present) ÷ 0.25, rounded up

With 84 of 120 days (70%), the student needs (90 − 84) ÷ 0.25 = 24 more days in a row: 108 of 144 is exactly 75%.

Days a student can still miss

Days you can miss = (days present − 0.75 × days held) ÷ 0.75, rounded down

Minimum attendance rules

  • CBSE: at least 75% attendance to appear in the Class 10 and 12 board exams. Shortages can be condoned only in specific cases, such as serious illness, through the school.
  • CISCE (ICSE and ISC): schools follow a 75% requirement for board exam candidates.
  • State boards: most use 75%, and some allow condonation down to a lower figure. Check your board’s current circular.

Catching a shortfall early matters more than the formula. A student at 72% in October can still recover. A student at 72% in February usually cannot.
