What a character certificate includes

A character certificate, also called a conduct certificate, states how long the student studied at the school, the last class they attended, and the school’s view of their conduct. Colleges and new schools usually ask for it along with the transfer certificate.

Choosing the conduct rating

Base the rating on the student’s records: disciplinary entries, the class teacher’s remarks and participation in school life. Most schools use Excellent, Very good, Good or Satisfactory. If there were serious disciplinary issues, the principal should decide the wording.
