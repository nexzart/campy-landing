How the dates are chosen

  1. The first paper falls on your start date, or the next school day if that is a Sunday or holiday.
  2. Each later paper leaves the number of study days you set, counting only school days.
  3. Sundays, holidays and, if you choose, Saturdays are never exam days.

Tips for a fair date sheet

  • Put the heaviest subjects, such as Mathematics and Science, after a weekend or study day.
  • Avoid two language papers back to back.
  • Leave a day free after a long holiday, so students aren’t tested on the day they return.
