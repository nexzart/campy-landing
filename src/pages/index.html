The problem

Schools deserve better.

Running a school involves much more than teaching. Attendance registers, exam records, timetables, homework, staff coordination, parent communication, and years of student records often end up spread across notebooks, spreadsheets, and messaging apps.

This creates:

  • Unnecessary workHours spent on registers and paperwork.
  • Repeated data entryThe same details copied into many places.
  • Missed updatesNotices that never reach the right people.
  • Records hard to findInformation missing when it matters.

The result?

More administrative pressure, less transparency, and valuable time taken away from teaching and learning.

Questions schools ask.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Does the price go up as we add students or staff?

No. Every plan is one annual price for unlimited students and unlimited staff: ₹35,000 for Essential, ₹1,00,000 for the Professional ERP and ₹2,00,000 for Enterprise with the school website. Prices exclude GST.

Is there a development cost?

No. Campy is ready-made, so there is nothing to build. We configure it for your school, and Professional and Enterprise plans include onboarding and data migration.

What is the 0.1% platform fee?

A fee of 0.1% of the fees your school collects through Campy, on every plan. Fees go straight to your school’s bank account through your own payment gateway, and gateway charges are separate.

Can we keep our accountant’s software and our payment gateway?

Yes. Campy works with any payment gateway that has an API, and exports vouchers to Tally and Excel. See the integrations page for the full list.

What if we run out of storage?

Plans include from about 40 GB to 250 GB. As your records grow, you can add storage at affordable rates instead of moving to a bigger student or staff package.

How long does it take to get started?

You set up your classes, students, staff, academic calendar and timetable, configure Campy to match how your school works, and then move daily operations into it. We walk you through it in your demo.

What happens if a teacher misses the attendance window?

They can’t enter it late directly. They send a correction request, and an administrator approves it. Both the request and the approval are logged.

What happens at the end of the academic year?

You decide, student by student, who is promoted, who repeats the class and who graduates. The year is archived, and graduates move to alumni records.

Does Campy write our curriculum or question papers?

No. Campy runs school operations and academic records: it manages exams and marks, but it doesn’t generate curriculum or exam questions.
