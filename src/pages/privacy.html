1. Who we are

Campy is built and run by Nexzart, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. This policy explains how we handle personal data in the Campy platform and apps, on this website, and in the free tools. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the rules made under it.

2. Schools and Nexzart

  • Inside Campy, the School decides what personal data to collect about its students, parents and staff, and why. The School is the data fiduciary, and Nexzart is its data processor: we handle that data only to provide the Service on the School’s instructions. Questions about a School’s records should go to the School first.
  • On this website, for example when you book a demo or subscribe to updates, Nexzart is the data fiduciary.

3. What we collect

From website visitors

  • Demo requests: your name, work email, phone number, school, role, school size, plan of interest and message.
  • Product updates: your email address.
  • Technical data that every web request carries, such as IP address, browser and country, kept in security logs.

In the Campy platform, on behalf of Schools

  • Student records: names, dates of birth, class, attendance, marks, health notes, guardian details, PEN and APAAR IDs where the School records them.
  • Parent and staff details: names, phone numbers, email addresses, roles, and, for staff, employment, bank and statutory details used for payroll.
  • Fee records: dues, receipts and payment status. Card and bank details are entered on the payment gateway’s pages and are not stored by Campy.
  • Usage and security data: sign-ins, devices and the audit log of changes.

In the free tools

The free tools run in your browser. What you type, paste or upload into them is not sent to us. If you allow preference cookies, your school letterhead and checklist ticks are remembered in your browser’s local storage, and nowhere else.

4. Children’s data

Most student records in Campy are about children. Schools are responsible for obtaining verifiable consent from a parent or guardian where the DPDP Act requires it. We do not use children’s data for advertising, behavioural monitoring or tracking, and we do not build profiles of children for any purpose other than the School’s own educational and administrative work.

5. How we use it

  • To provide, secure and support the Service for each School.
  • To send notifications, reminders and messages that the School has set up.
  • To reply to demo requests and send product updates you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To bill Schools, including the subscription and the platform fee on fee collections.
  • To meet legal obligations, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run Campy, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us:

  • Cloud hosting, storage and content delivery (for example Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare).
  • Google Workspace, to send the confirmation emails for demo requests and product update sign-ups.
  • Push notifications (Firebase), and the SMS, WhatsApp and email providers a School chooses.
  • Payment gateways chosen by the School, for fee payments, and Razorpay for our own invoices.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, for example to comply with a valid order from a court or authority.

7. Security

Each School’s data is isolated from every other School’s. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is controlled by role, sign-in uses one-time codes, important changes are written to an audit log, and data is backed up daily. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the affected School and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. How long we keep it

  • School Data: for as long as the School subscribes, then as described in our terms (export for 60 days, then deletion).
  • Demo requests: up to two years after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Security logs: for a limited period, normally no more than one year.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you, ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it, withdraw consent you have given, nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated, and raise a grievance. For data a School holds about you or your child, contact the School; we will help the School respond. For website data, write to us at the address below.

10. Cookies and local storage

We keep cookies and similar storage to a minimum, and we don’t use advertising cookies.

  • Essential: needed for security, form protection and to remember your cookie choice. These are always on.
  • Preferences: remember your school letterhead and checklist progress in the free tools. Only used if you allow them.
  • Analytics: anonymous statistics about which pages are useful. Only used if you allow them.

You can change your choice at any time: . Switching preferences off also clears what the free tools had stored.

11. Grievances and contact

Grievance Officer, Nexzart, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Email: admin@nexzart.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

We will post any changes to this policy here and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects Schools, we will tell them in advance.
