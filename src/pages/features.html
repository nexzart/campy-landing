Attendance

Registers that fit how your school takes attendance, and alerts that reach parents while it still matters.

If a teacher misses the 20-minute marking window, they send a correction request for approval instead of entering attendance late.
  • Half-day or period-wise. Choose per school how often registers are taken.
  • Marking windows. Set how long a register stays open after the period starts.
  • Absence alerts. Linked parents are notified according to your school’s rules.
  • History and trends. Look up any student, class or day, and spot patterns across the term.
  • Correction requests. Late or mistaken entries go through an approval, and both are logged.
  • Offline marking. Take the register without a connection; it syncs when you’re back online.

Exams and marks

From the exam schedule to the published result, with the class teacher deciding when parents see marks.

A parent sees their child’s marks only after the class teacher locks and publishes the results.
  • Create exams. Unit tests, terms and finals, by class and subject.
  • Assign staff. Choose correcting and invigilating teachers for each paper.
  • Marks entry. Teachers enter marks for the papers they correct, even offline.
  • Grades worked out for you. Percentages and grades are calculated from your grading scale.
  • Publish and unpublish. Release results when they’re locked, and pull them back if something needs fixing.

Timetable and academic calendar

Build the week, plan the year, and let Campy catch the clashes before anyone walks into the wrong room.

If one teacher is assigned to two classes at the same time, Campy warns the administrator and won’t save the timetable until it’s fixed.
  • Periods and breaks. Set timings once and reuse them across classes.
  • Working Saturdays and holidays. The calendar decides which days have registers.
  • Special events. Sports days, exams and functions on the school calendar parents see.
  • Timetable versions. Change the timetable mid-year and keep the previous one on record.
  • Conflict detection. Double-booked staff are flagged and invalid timetables can’t be saved.

Homework and announcements

One place for what’s due and what’s happening, instead of a dozen messaging groups.

Parents get a homework reminder one day before the due date.
  • Assignments with due dates. Posted to a class or subject, visible to students’ parents.
  • PDF attachments. Worksheets and notes travel with the assignment.
  • School announcements. Broadcast to the whole school, a class, or just staff.
  • Reminders. Sent automatically ahead of due dates.

Leave and substitutes

Leave requests with a clear status, and cover arranged before the period starts.

When a teacher’s leave is approved, the substitute sees the periods they need to cover on those dates.
  • Student leave. Parents apply from the parent portal and track the decision.
  • Staff leave. Teachers apply for their own leave from the staff portal.
  • Substitute assignment. Authorised admins assign cover for specific dates and periods.
  • Approvals on record. Every request keeps its history: who asked, who approved, when.

Academic year, promotion and alumni

Close one year and open the next without losing a single record.

At year-end, the school can see which students are promoted, staying in the same class, graduating, or still awaiting a decision.
  • Promotion. Move students to their next class in bulk or one at a time.
  • Repeating a class. Keep a student in the same class with their history intact.
  • Graduation and alumni. Graduates move to alumni records you can still look up.
  • Archived years. Attendance, marks and timetables from past years stay on record.

Checklists

The small things every office tracks, without a separate register for each.

A class teacher ticks off who has received their textbooks, and the office sees the status for the whole school.
  • Fee status. See at a glance who has paid for the term.
  • Book distribution. Track textbooks and materials handed out per student.
  • Form submissions. Consent forms, documents and anything else you collect.
  • Permissions. Choose which staff can update each checklist; parents see their child’s status.

Notifications and reports

Everyone has an inbox of what happened, and the office has the reports to act on it.

An administrator can see who changed a student’s marks, what the old value was, and when it changed.
  • Notification inbox. Every alert is kept, so nothing depends on catching a push notification.
  • Push notifications. On the Android and iOS apps.
  • Attendance trends. By student, class and term.
  • Audit logs. A record of actions and changes across the school.
  • Administrative reports. The summaries principals and management ask for.

What Campy doesn’t do

Campy runs school operations and academic records. It manages exams and curriculum-related administration, but it doesn’t write your curriculum, generate question papers, or judge teaching quality. Those stay with your academic team.
