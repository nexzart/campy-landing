Billing questions.

Is Campy priced per student or per teacher?

No. Campy is one annual platform fee for unlimited students and unlimited staff. Enrolling more students or hiring more staff doesn’t increase it.

How much storage is included?

Approximately 40 to 50 GB, according to the selected plan. That covers student records, academic archives and school documents.

What if we need more storage?

You can add storage at affordable rates as your records grow, instead of paying for a larger student or staff package. Additional-storage rates are confirmed in your plan.

Are taxes included?

Final pricing, storage allowance and taxes are confirmed in your selected plan before you sign.

How do we pay?

Invoices can be paid online through Razorpay.
