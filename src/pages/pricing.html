Billing questions.

Is there a development or setup cost?

No development cost on any plan. Campy is ready-made software that we configure for your school. Professional and Enterprise plans also include onboarding and data migration from your current registers or spreadsheets.

Is Campy priced per student or per teacher?

No. Every plan is one annual price for the whole school, with unlimited students and unlimited staff. Enrolling more students doesn’t change it.

What is the 0.1% platform fee?

A fee of 0.1% of the fees your school collects through Campy, on every plan. On ₹50 lakh of fees in a year, that is ₹5,000. It appears on a monthly statement and is separate from your payment gateway’s charges.

Can we use our own payment gateway?

Yes. Campy works with Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm, Cashfree, PayU, CCAvenue, Easebuzz, bank gateways and others, and money settles directly into your school’s bank account. If your gateway has an API, we can connect it.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. You can add modules or upgrade at any time and pay only the difference for the rest of your subscription year. Your data stays exactly where it is.

How much storage is included?

About 40 to 50 GB on Essential, 100 GB on Professional and 250 GB on Enterprise. You can add storage at affordable rates as your records grow.

Are taxes included?

Prices exclude GST, which is added to your invoice at the applicable rate. Final pricing is confirmed in your order before you sign.

How do we pay for Campy?

Annual invoices can be paid online through Razorpay, or by bank transfer.
