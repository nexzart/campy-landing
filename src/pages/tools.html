About these tools.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use as often as you like, with no account, trial or watermark.

Is student data uploaded anywhere?

No. Calculations, certificates and plans are produced in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or upload is sent to a server. If you allow preference cookies, your school’s name, address and logo are remembered in this browser, so you don’t have to retype them on each certificate.

How do I save a certificate or report as a PDF?

Press Print on the tool, then choose “Save as PDF” as the printer in your browser’s print dialog.

Can I use my school’s logo on documents?

Yes. Every document generator has a School details panel where you add your logo, name, address and affiliation number. They appear on the letterhead of every certificate, receipt and circular.

How is this different from Campy?

These tools work on one student, class or calculation at a time. Campy keeps your whole school’s attendance, exams, timetables and records in one system, so reports and certificates come straight from data you already have.
