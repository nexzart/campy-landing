One platform, many schools, no crossed wires.

Campy is a multi-school platform. Every school runs on the same product, while its students, staff and records stay its own.

Each school’s data stays separate

Records are isolated per school. Staff and parents only ever reach the school they belong to, and super admins manage schools rather than their records.

Access follows roles

Permissions decide who can mark, approve, publish and edit. A teacher sees assigned classes; a parent sees their own children; admins see what their role allows.

Every change is on record

Audit logs record actions and the relevant changes, so you can answer “who changed this, and when?” without guessing.

The urgent work works offline

Attendance and marks entry keep working without a connection and sync when the device reconnects.

Security and accountability

Built for children’s data.

Schools hold some of the most sensitive data there is. These protections come with every plan, including Essential.

  • OTP sign-inOne-time codes to the number on record. No passwords to share or forget.
  • Session managementSee every signed-in device and sign out a lost phone remotely.
  • Access controlRole-based permissions for every action, from marking to refunds.
  • Audit logsWho changed what, the old value and when.
  • Data isolationEach school’s records are kept apart from every other school’s.
  • Encryption and backupsEncrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily.
  • Privacy by designConsent records, data export and erasure, aligned with the DPDP Act, 2023. Privacy policy
  • Fees never pass through usPayments settle from your gateway straight to your school’s account.
